Privacy Policy for Better Days: Mood

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

Introduction

Better Days: Mood ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal information.

Data Collection

We collect the following information that you provide directly:

  • Profile Information: First name, last name, and gender (optional)
  • Mood Entries: Mood scores, text summaries, and timestamps
  • App Settings: Notification preferences and reminder times

How We Use Your Data

All data you enter in Better Days: Mood is:

  • Stored locally on your device only
  • Never transmitted to our servers or third parties
  • Never shared with anyone
  • Never used for advertising or tracking

Your data is used solely to:

  • Display your mood history and insights
  • Calculate statistics and trends
  • Send local notification reminders (if enabled)

Data Storage

  • All data is stored locally on your iOS device using Apple's SwiftData framework
  • No cloud syncing or remote backups are performed by the app
  • Your data is protected by your device's security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID)

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • You have full control over your data
  • You can delete individual mood entries at any time
  • You can export your data in CSV or JSON format
  • You can permanently delete all app data from Settings > Delete All Data
  • Uninstalling the app will permanently remove all local data

Third-Party Services

Better Days: Mood does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all your data within the app
  • Export your data at any time
  • Delete your data at any time
  • Opt-out of notifications

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
